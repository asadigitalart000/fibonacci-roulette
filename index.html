House edge is 2.7% on single-zero and 5.26% on double-zero wheels. No staking system changes expected value.

#No.HitBetsRoundNet
How it works. After each spin, tap the number. Two sides are tracked separately: dozens (1st/2nd/3rd 12) and columns (2 to 1). On each side a block qualifies when it missed both of the last 2 spins; if 2+ qualify, bet the two coldest (longest since last hit across the log; ties go to the lower block). So a round has 0, 2 or 4 bets. Each of the 6 blocks has its own Fibonacci ladder (1,1,2,3,5,8…): stake = its ladder value × unit. A bet block that hits nets +2× its stake and resets to 1; a bet block that misses (zeros included) loses its stake and steps up one rung; unbet blocks keep their ladder.

Zero hedge (optional): Z covers 0 (35:1) or the 0/00 split (17:1), placed only on rounds where any bet is live. Each such round without a zero is a hedge loss; after 23 in a row Z goes up 1 unit; any zero resets Z to 1 unit and the count to 0.

Can cover N more losses assumes the current (or next most likely) bets all keep losing, each stepping up its own ladder.
